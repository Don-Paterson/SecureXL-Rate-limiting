Penalty Box settings

Applied to both members

A source whose traffic is dropped faster than the threshold is put in the Penalty Box. SecureXL then drops all its packets early until the timeout expires. These settings are global to the gateway, not specific to A-DMZ.

Deny list

Blocks all traffic to and from an address

This is a broader control than rate limiting. It blocks the source everywhere, not only towards A-DMZ, so it can hide the rate-limit behaviour during a test. Entries added here do not survive a reboot. For a permanent entry, use the files in $FWDIR/conf/deny_lists/.
